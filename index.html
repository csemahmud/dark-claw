About Dark Claw

Dark Claw is an original fitness cosplay character designed by Khan Mahmudul Hasan (カーン), a cosplayer based in Japan. The project combines strength training, weightlifting, long-distance cycling, cosplay event appearances, photography, and original character design into an independent cosplay portfolio. Dark Claw made its public debut at Comic Market (Comiket) 107 in Japan.

About the Project

Dark Claw is an independent original cosplay project developed by Khan Mahmudul Hasan in Japan. The portfolio documents costume development, fitness progress, photoshoots, and different cosplay events appearances.
